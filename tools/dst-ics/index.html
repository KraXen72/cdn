Clock change reminders

This calendar adds yearly reminders at 20:00 (Europe/Amsterdam) on the evening before Central Europe’s daylight saving time changes. Each event tells you whether the clocks go forward or back that night.

Calendar URL

https://kraxen72.github.io/cdn/tools/dst-ics/dst.ics

Add to Google Calendar

  1. Open Google Calendar in a computer browser.
  2. Next to Other calendars, click +, then From URL.
  3. Paste the URL above and click Add calendar.
