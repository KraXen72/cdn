# Bible Reading Plan | Part 1 of 4

**Weeks 01-26 | 2 Nov 2026 to 2 May 2027**

> [!info] How to use this plan
> Check off each passage when you have read it. The date ranges indicate the target week, not mandatory weekdays or deadlines.
> Each week has five readings except Week 104, which has three. The 4-4-5 week "months" are organizational rather than calendar months.
> Book names follow the Slovak Ecumenical Translation. Context notes are editorial, not part of the publisher's plan.
> Source: [Blue Letter Bible, original numbered two-year plan](https://www.blueletterbible.org/assets-v3/pdf/dbrp/2Yr_CanonicalFiveDayPlan.pdf).
